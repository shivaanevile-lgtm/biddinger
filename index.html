How Dr4fter works

30 seconds and you're playing.

1. Pick a theme. Free-for-all lists, position drafts (5-a-side, pizza, movie), or a club rivalry.
2. Everyone gets $20 and 5 slots. Items come up one at a time.
3. Bid or pass. Raise by any amount you can afford. Highest bid wins the lot and pays exactly that.
4. Run out of money? Once you're at exactly $0, remaining lots you still need come to you free.
5. Best collection wins. Every item has a 1–10 rating — spending more doesn't win, picking better does.
